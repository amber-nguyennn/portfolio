The Heady Day

A content channel about moving from Hanoi to Gothenburg to study - scholarships, visas, cost of living, and what nobody tells you before you land. Grown organically to millions of views across short-form platforms.

Why I started it

Applying for a Swedish Institute scholarship from Vietnam meant piecing together advice from scattered forum posts and outdated blogs. I started documenting my own process in public - partly as a record, partly because the questions in my inbox kept repeating.

How I run it

Same loop I use at work: watch what audiences actually ask, prioritise by reach and effort, ship, read the numbers, repeat. Threads carries long-form questions and discussion; TikTok carries the visual, first-week-in-Sweden material.
